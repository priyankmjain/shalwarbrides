Our Story

About Shalwar Brides

Shalwar Brides is Sri Lanka's ultimate destination for bridal and ethnic ladies wear, with showrooms in Colombo Pettah and Negombo. We are passionate about helping every woman — especially brides — find an outfit that makes them feel extraordinary.

Our collection is thoughtfully curated from luxury Banarasi sarees to exquisite lehengas, each piece selected with dedication to quality, uniqueness, and affordability.

We believe affordable luxury is not a compromise — it is our promise. Every customer is treated with warmth, patience, and genuine care.
